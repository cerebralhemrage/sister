A Poem for My Sister

I watched you grow from sister
to something even more—
a mother fierce and radiant,
a light I can't ignore.
You carry chaos beautifully,
with laughter and with grit,
and somehow make the hard days look
like you were born for it.
The patience and the wildness,
the tenderness, the play—
you give your kids the kind of love
that doesn't look away.
So here's a cheer from someone
who's watched you all along:
you're the kind of mother, Kels,
they'll write into a song.

— your proud sibling

Tap the bud to make it bloom

Motherhood looks incredible on you.

Tap to reveal

Sis to sis

Your kids have no idea how lucky they are. But I do.

Real talk

You make the hard stuff look easy and the easy stuff magical.

Always

You were my first best friend and my favorite mom to watch in action.

You deserve a celebration

go ahead, you've earned it

Happy Mother's Day, superstar!

Make a wish

tap the dandelion and send it off

Wishing you rest, joy, and all the hugs you can handle.

To My Incredible Sister

Love you to pieces.
